# Team Competition: UNIBOTS
`February 2026 - April 2026`

## Description

UNIBOTS is a UK team competition where students from different universities compete against each other in a robotics challenge. The competition is focused around making an autonomous robot that can detect, capture and drop balls in a specific area while being confronted to other robots in the arena. [unibots.uk](https://unibots.uk/)

## Until the test day (2 months)

The school already had multiple teams participating in the competition, but with some friends from my MSc program, we decided to create our own team "UniBaguette". We started late (February 2026) compared to the other teams (November 2025), but we were able to catch up by the time of the first test day (April 2026). At that time, there were 6 of us but the main contributors were 3 of us. Antoine was more of the team leader, Thomas was the hardware expert and I was the designer.

At the test day we managed to have a robot that could move around with a first idea of ball capturing and dropping. We did not seem behind most teams which made us quite satisfied with our progress.

### My contribution

I invented some methods to capture and drop the balls together with a 4th member, Maxime. I presented the different ideas to the team and bit by bit we ended up agreeing on a final design. As there was no time to make a clean 3D printed prototype before the test day, I designed the first version mainly using wood. This took some days of work but it was worth it as we were able to test the design and make improvements before the final version. This helped the team to make a clean 3D printed version of the design that was more efficient and robust once I had to leave the team.

## After the test day (2 months)

After the test day, I sadly had to leave the team due to personal reasons. I continued giving them some advice and support, especially by explaining the design choices I made and the reasoning behind them. The team continued to work on the robot and managed to improve it significantly. They were able to capture and drop balls more efficiently and even managed to earn the third place in the competition. I am very proud of their achievement and I am glad to have been part of the team, even if only for a short time.
