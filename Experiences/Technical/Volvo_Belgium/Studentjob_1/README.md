# 4 months at Volvo Cars Ghent as a student in robotics and automation
`2nd of September 2024 - 17th of January 2025`

## About the team

I was part of a team of engineers in the body shop (the part of the factory where the car body is assembled). The main focus of this team was to ensure that the production line was running smoothly and efficiently. My role was to take over one of the projects that was being worked on by another engineer who had too many projects to handle. Next to this project, I continued staying in touch with my previous colleagues from my internship in Sweden.

## My tasks

### I. Led development and deployment of Volvo Cars’ first movable collaborative robot

The idea of this project was to deploy a collaborative robot (COBOT) in the body shop. The COBOT was fixed on what was called the CUBE so that it could be moved around the factory. The main goal of this project was to create a versatile and flexible solution for the body shop. The COBOT was to be used for various tasks and could be moved to different locations in the factory as needed.


#### a. Coordination of players

As I had to lead this project, I coordinated technicians, external inspectors, suppliers and stakeholders with the goal of deploying a collaborative robot in the body shop. It was a challenging task, as I had to ensure that all players were on the same page and that the project was progressing according to plan. I had to coordinate:
- robot technicians: the ones doing most of the hands-on work
- electrical experts: have them draw a schematic for the electrical cabinet for future inspections
- external inspectors: have them check the safety of the COBOT and the CUBE on both the mechanical and electrical side
- production managers: have them approve the project and receive production items to test the COBOT on
- external safety experts: co-author the safety documentation package and have it approved by senior management
- senior management: reporting to them on the progress of the project, work towards their demands and have them approve the safety documentation package


#### b. Safety documentation package

I co-authored the full safety documentation package for the COBOT and the CUBE. An external safety expert was hired to help us with this task, as it was the first time that a COBOT was deployed in the body shop. I scheduled weekly or bi-weekly meetings with him to discuss the progress of the safety documentation package and to ensure that we were on the right track. He told me what documents were needed and what information was required for each document. I then had to gather this information from the different players involved in the project and write the documents.


#### c. Training of line operators and technicians

The COBOT was to be implemented in the body shop during the Christmas shutdown, which meant that I had to train the line operators and technicians on how to use the COBOT once they got back to work. I created training materials and scheduled training sessions for the line operators and technicians by coordinating with the production managers and the technicians' managers themselves. This was a hard task since there were four different shifts (morning, evening, night, and weekend) and I had to make sure that all operators were trained. The training sessions would be held in small groups and led by myself and one of the robot technicians in case of technical questions. Unfortunately, a problem arose with the COBOT during the Christmas shutdown, which meant that the COBOT was not ready to be used when the line operators and technicians came back to work. As I stayed for only 2 more weeks there was no time and I had to leave the training sessions to the robot technicians. I created a training manual for them to use during the training sessions, which included all the information that I would have given them during the training sessions.


#### d. COBOT presentation to stakeholders and line operators

Together with one of the robot technicians, I presented the COBOT to the stakeholders by doing a live demonstration of the COBOT in action. Afterwards, we saw the oppotunity to present the COBOT to the line operators by showing it to them during a shift switch. I added a presentation for them explaining what the COBOT was, what it could do, and how it would help them in their work.


#### e. Some technical tasks

I ended up doing some technical tasks as well such as programming for the demonstration but also:
- verifying the mechanical design and stability report of the CUBE. The external company's certification seemed a bit off and not detailled or explained enough. I redid the whole stability analysis myself, had a call with the external company and made an improved stability report by detailing the calculations and explaining the results.
- 3D designing and printing of some parts for protecting future operators from the COBOT.
- labeling all the parts of the COBOT and CUBE including the electrical cabinet for the external inspectors to be able to check the safety of the COBOT and CUBE.
- testing out all the COBOT's functions and making sure that it was working properly in order to make a good instruction manual for the line operators and technicians.


#### f. Difficulties faced

The main difficulties I faced were:
- Telling experienced and older technicians what to do although I was a student
- Telling people what to do although most of them were entirely occupied by their own tasks (most of them could only work on the project in overtime)
- Having different bosses with different priorities agree on the same project and timeline
- Creating a full safety package for a COBOT for the first time in the company, which required a lot of research and learning about safety standards and regulations
- Not being allowed to do much myself as I was a student, which meant that I had to rely on others to do the work. I would have loved to do more hands-on work, but I had to focus on coordinating and leading the project.


### II. Representative of the plant in the Global COBOT network

While I was in Sweden a few months earlier, a Global COBOT network between all the Volvo plants was created. Now in Ghent, I became the representative of the plant in this network. Ghent was Volvo Cars' leading plant in the COBOT field, and I had to share my experience with the other plants and learn from their experiences as well. Especially, I had to share my experience with the safety documentation package to help the other plants with their own COBOT projects. Information about the network and the other plants' COBOT projects was shared through a Teams channel and through meetings.


### III. Co-organiser of an international COBOT supplier evaluation event

My Swedish colleagues from the Headquarters wanted to hold an international COBOT supplier evaluation event to help them select the best COBOT supplier for future projects. As Ghent was the leading plant in the COBOT field, I was asked to co-organise this event. I had to coordinate with my Swedish colleagues and the suppliers to organise the event. I prepared a walkthrough of the Body Shop with specific use cases to challenge the suppliers and later hear individually how each of them would solve the use cases. As their are three parts in the plants (body shop, paint shop, and assembly), I coordinated with the other two parts of the plant to organise a similar event for them.


## Conclusion

I had a great time working at Volvo Cars Ghent as a student in robotics and automation. I learned a lot about project management, coordination, and safety standards. I also had the opportunity to work on a challenging and exciting project that allowed me to apply my skills and knowledge in a real-world setting.
