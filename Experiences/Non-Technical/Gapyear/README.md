# WWOOFing on Vancouver Island before crossing through Canada by bike
`2nd of February 2025 - 28th of July 2025`

## Project Overview

With a friend of mine we wanted to do a gap year before finishing our studies in order to experience and live one of our dreams. The main goal was to discover Canada by bike and to meet new people and cultures. We could go to Canada for 6 months with our passports but had to be back end July to prepare for our last study year. Thus we decided to do a gap year from February to July 2025. However, Canada is a country with tons of snow and ice in the winter, so we decided to only start our trip begin April in Vancouver. For the first 2 months we wanted to do some WWOOFing experiences on Vancouver Island to get to know the country and its culture but also to learn about sustainable farming and permaculture. After that we would start our bike trip from Vancouver Island to Halifax, crossing the country from west to east.

I spent on average 2 hours per day on a live blog to document our experiences and to share them with friends and family. You can find the blog here: [farming-and-biking-across-canada](https://www.polarsteps.com/ArneJacobs02/16045408-farming-and-biking-across-canada?s=9bf56fc5-54e6-4999-9efe-8978b34e3647).

## WWOOFing Experiences

### Pender Island

The first month we stayed on Pender Island with Chris. As it was a bit early for the season, we had to do some work in the garden (pruning apple trees for example) and on the fence to prepare for the upcoming season. We stayed in a small wooden cabin with a bed and a stove and an outdoor toilet and shower. We cooked our meals ourselves but were provided with groceries and his own eggs and meat. We also had the opportunity to meet some of his friends and neighbors and to learn about their way of life. On our free time we went on a few hikes and explored the island. On this small island there was one of Canada's biggest disk golf courses and we had the chance to try out this amazing sport for the first time.

### Victoria Highlands

The second month we stayed in Victoria Highlands on a gigantic property owned by a lovely elderly couple. They had some more work for us to do and we learned a lot about permaculture and sustainable farming! It was a very interesting experience were we:
- cut wood for the fire and learned about the different types of wood and their uses
- removed some invasive plants
- helped with groceries
- prepared a variety of beds for planting and sowing seeds for lots of different vegetables and herbs
- learned about the different types of plants and their needs
- learned about the different types of soil and how to improve it
- helped preparing the compost and learned about the different types of composting
- cleaned out the goat barn and kept an eye on some newly born goats
- helped transfer 3 highland cows to a new pasture while keeping them on track using our bodies and voices

Apart from that work we also integrated in the family, ate with them and learned about their way of life. One of the highlights was a Traditional Scottish Ceilidh dancing event that happened nearby once a year. We had an incredible time dancing with the locals and exchanging with them! We also played a bit of pickle ball, a sport that is a mix between tennis and ping pong, for the first time.

On our free time we also went on a few hikes and explored the area inside and around the property. We also went to Victoria to explore the city and buy final necessities for our upcoming bike trip. 

### Chilliwack

We left Victoria near the end of March, biked to Vancouver where we stayed a night and then biked to Chilliwack where we did a last 5 days of WWOOFing with a woodworker. We unfortunately did not have a lot of wood work to help with but helped him clean up his whole property and his workshop. We removed invasive plants, cut the grass, cleaned the workshop, outdoor stones and the driveway. These 5 days were the physically most demanding of the WWOOFing experiences but as they cooked amazing meals for us and we had a lot of fun with them, it was a very nice experience! We also learned a lot about woodworking through the different tools and machines he had in his workshop and his creations all over the house.


## Bike Trip

We biked from Vancouver to Halifax, crossing the country from west to east with about 7200km in 3.5 months. During this whole trip we did not use one hotel in order to keep the costs low and to be able to meet more people. We used a mix of wild camping and warmshowers. Warmshowers is a platform where people can offer cyclotourists a place to shower and sleep for free. We discovered Warmshowers and it is one of the best things that happened to us during this trip. We met so many amazing people that also love cyclotourism, have great stories to share, are friendly and welcoming and are always happy to help. Often, they would even prepare dinner and breakfast for us!

The trip was from Victoria to Halifax by crossing the Rocky Mountains, the prairies, Lake Superior, the Canadian Shield, Niagara Falls, Gaspesie, Bay of Fundy and the eastern beaches. We had to face a lot of challenges during this trip, from the weather, the terrain, the physical and mental challenges, but we also had a lot of fun and learned a lot about ourselves and the world around us. We met so many amazing people and had unforgettable experiences that we will remember for the rest of our lives.
