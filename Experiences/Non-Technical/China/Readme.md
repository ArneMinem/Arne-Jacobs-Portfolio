# Summer camp in China
`11th of May 2026 - 25th of May 2026`

While studying at Heriot-Watt University in Edinburgh, I got selected to participate in a summer camp in China organised by the Scottish Confucius Institute for Business & Communication. The camp was held in Tianjin, a city near Beijing, and lasted for  weeks. During the camp, we had the opportunity to learn about Chinese culture, history, and language. We also visited a company and spent days at a university in China, which gave us a better understanding of the business environment in the country.

I was chosen by the main organiser of the camp to be the leader of the group of students from Heriot-Watt University. I was responsible for checking if everyone was enjoying the activities and if they were having a good time. Everyone had a great time during the camp, so I did not have to do much, but I was happy to help and make sure everyone was having a good time. I also had the opportunity to meet and interact with students from other universities in China, which was a great experience.

## 11 days in Tianjin

During the 11 days in Tianjin, we had daily language classes in the morning at the Tianjin University and activities in the afternoon. The activities included visits to the city center (Tianjin Eye, Ancient Culture Street, Italian Style Street, etc.), a chinese opera show, a visit to a drone show company, a tea ceremony, a calligraphy class, dumpling making, dressing in traditional chinese clothes, etc.

At the end of these 11 days, we had to pass a HSK test (Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, 汉语水平考试) to evaluate our chinese language skills. I passed the HSK 1 test with a score of 100/100. Although HSK 1 is the lowest level of the HSK test, I was very happy to have passed it with a perfect score!

## 3 days in Beijing

During the last 3 days of the camp, we visited Beijing. We had the opportunity to visit the Great Wall of China, the Forbidden City, and the Temple of Heaven. We also had a guided tour of the city, which allowed us to learn more about the history and culture of Beijing. The visit to Beijing was a great way to end the summer camp in China, and I am very grateful for having had this opportunity.
